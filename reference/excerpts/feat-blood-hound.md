# Blood Hound (Origin Feat)

Source: Player's Handbook (2024), not in this repo's bundled SRD. Provided by the player from their own source.

Your senses are heightened beyond that of most folk. Whether these senses were strengthened through training, the loss of other senses, or they simply matured as you did, you are a master of finding those whom you seek. You gain the following benefits:

**Motion Sensor.** Whenever a creature that is Small or larger moves within 10 feet of you while you do not have the Unconscious condition, you immediately become aware of its presence.

**No Hiding.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sound or smell.

## Where it's used

Graven Fel's origin feat, taken via the 2024 Human's Versatile trait.
