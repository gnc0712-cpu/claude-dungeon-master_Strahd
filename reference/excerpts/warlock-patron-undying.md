# Warlock Patron: The Undying

Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014 rules), player-provided excerpt. Not in the bundled SRD.

Death holds no sway over your patron, who has unlocked the secrets of everlasting life, although such a prize—like all power—comes at a price. Once mortal, the Undying has seen mortal lifetimes pass like the seasons, like the flicker of endless days and nights. It has the secrets of the ages to share, secrets of life and death. Beings of this sort include Vecna, Lord of the Hand and the Eye; the dread Iuz; the lich-queen Vol; the Undying Court of Aerenal; Vlaakith, lich-queen of the githyanki; and the deathless wizard Fistandantalus. In the Realms, Undying patrons include Larloch the Shadow King, legendary master of Warlock's Crypt, and Gilgeam, the God-King of Unther.

## Among the Dead (1st level)

You learn the *spare the dying* cantrip (counts as a warlock cantrip for you). Advantage on saving throws against disease.

Undead have difficulty harming you: if an undead targets you directly with an attack or harmful spell, it must succeed on a Wisdom save (DC = your spell save DC) or choose a new target/forfeit the attack instead (doesn't apply to area effects like a *fireball*'s explosion that merely includes you). On a success, that creature is immune to this effect from you for 24 hours — same if you target it with an attack/harmful spell first.

## Defy Death (6th level)

Regain 1d8 + Constitution modifier (min. 1) HP when you succeed on a death saving throw, or when you stabilize a creature with *spare the dying*. Once per long rest.

## Undying Nature (10th level)

Hold your breath indefinitely; no longer require food, water, or sleep (still need rest to remove exhaustion, still benefit from short/long rests). Age at 1/10th the normal rate; immune to magical aging.

## Indestructible Life (14th level)

Bonus action: regain 1d8 + warlock level HP. If you put a severed body part back in place when using this, it reattaches. Once per short or long rest.
