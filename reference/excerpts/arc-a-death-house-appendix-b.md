# Death House — Appendix B (original Curse of Strahd module)

Source: original *Curse of Strahd* module, Appendix B (pp. 212–217+), provided by the player from their own copy. Captured in full since Reloaded's Arc A rebuilds almost the entire arc on top of this appendix room by room. **Reloaded overrides/additions take precedence wherever the two conflict** — see `reference/curse-of-strahd-reloaded/Act I - Into the Mists/Arc A - Escape From Death House.md` for what's changed (cultists, the family portrait with Thorn's doll, the grandfather clock, etc.).

## Death House's Features

Death House is aware of its surroundings and all creatures within it. Its goal is to continue the work of the cult by luring visitors to their doom.

The house has four stories (including the attic), with two balconies on the third floor — one facing the front, one facing the back. Wooden floors throughout; all windows have hinges that swing outward.

Rooms on the first and second floors are free of dust and signs of age — floorboards and wall panels well oiled, drapes and wallpaper unfaded, furniture new. The third floor and attic are dusty, drafty, and cobwebbed; floorboards groan underfoot.

Ceiling heights: 1st floor 10 ft., 2nd floor 12 ft., 3rd floor 8 ft., attic 13 ft.

None of the rooms are lit when the characters arrive, though most contain working oil lamps or fireplaces.

Characters can burn the house down if they want, but destruction is temporary — after 1d10 days it begins repairing itself (2d6 hours to complete). Items taken from the house aren't replaced, nor are destroyed undead. The dungeon level isn't part of the house and doesn't repair itself this way.

## Areas 1–10

**1. Entrance.** A wrought-iron gate (unlocked, shrieking rusty hinges) fills a stone portico archway (1A). Oil lamps flank oaken doors into a grand foyer (1B). South wall: a shield with a coat of arms (stylized golden windmill on red field) flanked by portraits of long-dead Dursts. Mahogany double doors with stained glass lead to the main hall (2A).

**2. Main Hall.** A wide hall (2A) with a black marble fireplace at one end and a sweeping red marble staircase at the other. A nonmagical longsword with a windmill cameo hilt is mounted above the fireplace. Wood-paneled walls sculpted with vines, flowers, nymphs, satyrs — DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) reveals serpents and skulls woven into the design. Paneling follows the staircase up to the second floor. A cloakroom (2B) has black cloaks on hooks and a top hat on a high shelf.

**3. Den of Wolves.** Oak-paneled hunter's den — stag's head above the fireplace, three stuffed wolves around the room. Two fur-draped padded chairs face the hearth, an oak table between them with a wine cask, two carved goblets, a pipe rack, a candelabrum. A chandelier hangs above a cloth-covered table with four chairs. East cabinet (locked, DC 15 Dex with thieves' tools) holds a heavy crossbow, light crossbow, hand crossbow, and 20 bolts each. North cabinet (unlocked) holds playing cards and wine glasses.

*Trapdoor:* hidden in the southwest corner, supernaturally concealed until approached from underneath (area 32).

**4. Kitchen and Pantry.** Tidy kitchen (4A) — dishware/cookware on shelves, worktable with cutting board and rolling pin, dome-shaped stone oven. Thin door to a well-stocked pantry (4B); food looks fresh but tastes bland.

*Dumbwaiter:* behind a small door in the kitchen's southwest corner — a 2-ft.-wide stone shaft with a rope-pulley elevator box, connecting to 7A and 12A, with a bell wired to buttons in those rooms. A Small character can squeeze in with DC 10 Dex (Acrobatics). Mechanism supports 200 lbs.

**5. Dining Room.** Carved mahogany table, eight high-backed chairs, crystal chandelier, silverware/crystalware polished to a shine. Mahogany-framed painting of an alpine vale above the marble fireplace. Wall paneling carved with deer among trees — DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) reveals twisted faces in the tree trunks and wolves lurking in the foliage. Red silk drapes; a tapestry of hunting dogs and mounted aristocrats chasing a wolf hangs on the south wall. Silverware tarnishes, crystal cracks, portrait fades, tapestry rots if removed from the house.

**6. Upper Hall.** Unlit oil lamps on the walls. Above the mantelpiece: a portrait of Gustav and Elisabeth Durst with their two smiling children, Rose and Thorn — the father cradles a swaddled baby the mother regards with scorn. Suits of armor (spear, wolf-head visored helm) flank doors in the east and west walls; the doors are carved with figures that look like dancing youths but (DC 12 Wisdom Perception) are actually fighting off swarms of bats. The red marble staircase continues up to area 11; a cold draft comes down the steps.

**7. Servants' Room.** Undecorated bedroom (7A) — two straw-mattress beds, empty footlockers at the foot of each. Servants' uniforms hang in the adjoining closet (7B). Dumbwaiter in the west wall corner with a button that rings the bell in 4A.

**8. Library.** Red velvet drapes. Mahogany desk and matching chair face the entrance and fireplace, above which hangs a framed picture of a windmill on a rocky crag. Two overstuffed chairs in the corners. Floor-to-ceiling bookshelves along the south wall with a rolling ladder. Desk holds an oil lamp, ink jar, quill, tinderbox, and a letter kit (red wax candle, four blank parchment sheets, wooden seal with the Durst windmill insignia). Desk drawer holds an iron key that unlocks area 20. Bookshelves hold hundreds of tomes (history, warfare, alchemy, first-edition poetry/fiction) — they rot if taken from the house.

*Secret Door:* behind a bookshelf, opened by pulling a switch disguised as a red-covered blank-spine book (DC 13 Wisdom Perception to spot). Springs close it automatically unless propped open. Leads to area 9.

**9. Secret Room.** Bookshelves of fiend-summoning and necromantic ritual tomes for a cult called the Priests of Osybus — the rituals are bogus (1 hour study + DC 12 Int Arcana to determine). A heavy wooden chest with clawed iron feet stands against the south wall, a skeleton in leather armor sticking out of it (a human killed by a now-nonfunctional poisoned dart trap, three darts in its armor/ribcage). The skeleton clutches a letter sealed by Strahd von Zarovich:

> *My most pathetic servant,*
>
> *I am not a messiah sent to you by the Dark Powers of this land. I have not come to lead you on a path to immortality. However many souls you have bled on your hidden altar, however many visitors you have tortured in your dungeon, know that you are not the ones who brought me to this beautiful land. You are but worms writhing in my earth.*
>
> *You say that you are cursed, your fortunes spent. You abandoned love for madness, took solace in the bosom of another woman, and sired a stillborn son. Cursed by darkness? Of that I have no doubt. Save you from your wretchedness? I think not. I much prefer you as you are.*
>
> *Your dread lord and master,*
> *Strahd von Zarovich*

*Treasure:* three blank black-leather books (25 gp each), three spell scrolls (*bless*, *protection from poison*, *spiritual weapon*), the deed to the house, the deed to a windmill (in the mountains east of Vallaki — Old Bonegrinder), and a signed will (Gustav and Elisabeth Durst bequeathing everything to Rosavalda and Thornboldt). Items age markedly if removed from the house but stay intact.

**10. Conservatory.** Gossamer drapes, brass-plated chandelier, upholstered chairs along the walls, stained-glass hangings of people singing/playing instruments. A harpsichord with bench in the northwest corner; a standing harp near the fireplace. Alabaster dancer figurines on the mantelpiece — close inspection shows several are carved as skeletons.

## Areas 11–20

**11. Balcony.** At the top of the red marble staircase, a dusty balcony with a cobweb-draped suit of black plate armor (animated armor — attacks when damaged or approached within 5 ft.; fights to destruction). Oak-paneled walls carved with woodland scenes — DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) reveals tiny corpses hanging from the trees and worms bursting from the ground.

*Secret Door:* west wall, DC 15 Wisdom (Perception). Opens easily to a cobweb-filled staircase up to the attic.

**12. Master Suite.** Double doors with dusty windmill-patterned stained glass. Master bedroom (12A): burgundy drapes, four-poster bed with embroidered curtains/tattered veils, matching empty wardrobes, vanity with wood-framed mirror and jewelry box, padded chair, rotting tiger-skin rug, dust-covered portrait of Gustav and Elisabeth above the fireplace. Web-filled parlor in the southwest corner: table, two chairs, dusty tablecloth with an empty porcelain bowl and jug. Full-length mirror door reveals an empty closet (12B). Parlor door leads to a balcony (12C).

*Dumbwaiter:* west wall corner, button rings the bell in 4A.

*Treasure:* silver/gold-filigree jewelry box (75 gp) containing three gold rings (25 gp each) and a platinum necklace with a topaz pendant (750 gp).

**13. Bathroom.** Dark room: wooden clawfoot tub, small iron stove with a kettle, barrel under a spigot (plumbing no longer works).

**14. Storage Room.** Dusty shelves (folded sheets, blankets, old soap). A cobweb-covered broom of animated attack leans against the far wall — attacks anything within 5 ft.

**15. Nursemaid's Suite.** Dust and cobwebs over an elegant bedroom (15A) and adjoining nursery (15B). Stained-glass double doors open to a front-facing balcony (15C). Backstory: the master of the house had an affair with the nursemaid, resulting in a stillborn baby (Walter); the cult killed the nursemaid shortly after. Unless already defeated in area 18, her spirit haunts this room as a specter — manifests and attacks when the nursery door opens; resembles a terrified, skeletally thin young woman, can't speak or be reasoned with. Bedroom: large bed, two end tables, empty wardrobe, full-length mirror framed in carved ivy/berries (DC 12 Wisdom Perception reveals eyeballs among the berries — secret door behind it). Nursery: a shrouded crib with a wrapped baby-sized bundle that's empty when unwrapped.

*Secret Door:* behind the mirror, DC 15 Wisdom (Perception). Opens to a cobweb-filled staircase up to the attic.

**16. Attic Hall.** Bare, dust- and cobweb-choked hall.

*Locked Door:* area 20's door is padlocked; key is in the library (area 8), or DC 15 Dex with thieves' tools.

**17. Spare Bedroom.** Dust-choked: slender bed, nightstand, small iron stove, writing desk with stool, empty wardrobe, rocking chair. A smiling doll in a lacy yellow dress sits in the north window box, cobwebbed like a wedding veil.

**18. Storage Room.** Dusty, packed with sheet-draped old furniture. Near an iron stove, under a sheet, an unlocked wooden trunk holds the nursemaid's skeletal remains wrapped in a bloodstained bedsheet (DC 14 Wisdom Medicine confirms she was stabbed to death). Disturbing the remains triggers her specter (unless already defeated in area 15).

*Secret Door:* east wall, appears only under conditions tied to area 21.

**19. Spare Bedroom.** Web-filled: slender bed, nightstand, rocking chair, empty wardrobe, small iron stove.

**20. Children's Room.** Door locked from outside (area 16). Bricked-up window flanked by two child-sized dusty beds. Toy chest painted with windmills; a dollhouse that's a perfect replica of the house itself. Two small skeletons lie on the floor in tattered recognizable clothing — Rose and Thorn Durst, who starved to death after being neglected and locked in this room; Thorn's skeleton cradles his stuffed doll. Toy chest holds stuffed animals/toys. DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) searching the dollhouse finds all the house's secret doors, including the attic one leading to the spiral staircase (area 21).

*Rose and Thorn:* if the dollhouse or chest is disturbed, their ghosts appear (use Monster Manual ghost stats, modified: Small, lawful good, 35 (10d6) HP each, no Horrifying Visage action, speak Common, CR 3/700 XP). They fight only in self-defense, know they're dead, and explain they were locked in the attic to protect them from "the monster in the basement" — Rose points to the dollhouse: "There's a secret door in the attic" (searching it afterward grants advantage on the Perception check to find secret doors). They fear abandonment — if characters try to leave, the ghosts attempt to possess them; a possessed character keeps player control but gains a flaw (Rose: "I like being in charge and get angry when other people tell me what to do"; Thorn: "I'm scared of everything, including my own shadow, and weep with despair when things don't go my way"). A possessed character won't willingly leave the house or dungeon; the ghost can be intimidated out with DC 11 Charisma (Intimidation) as an action. A ghost reduced to 0 HP reforms at the next dawn. The only way to lay them to rest permanently is placing their remains in their crypts (23E, 23F) — they don't know this themselves.

*Development:* if the party lays the children's spirits to rest, each character gains inspiration.

## Area 21

**21. Secret Stairs.** A creaky wooden spiral staircase in a 5-ft.-wide mortared stone shaft, descending 50 ft. from the attic to the dungeon level, cobwebbed (5-ft. visibility). The staircase/shaft don't exist until the house reveals them — either by the characters finding Strahd's letter (area 9) or the replica secret door in the dollhouse's attic (area 20). Once revealed, found automatically (no check) on searching the wall. Leads to area 22.

## Dungeon Features

Carved from earth, clay, and rock. Tunnels 4 ft. wide, 7 ft. high, timber-braced every 5 ft. Rooms 8 ft. tall on thick wooden posts/crossbeams, except area 38 (16 ft. ceiling, stone pillars). Unlit — non-darkvision characters need their own light. Centuries-old human footprints run every which way.

**22. Dungeon Level Access.** The spiral staircase ends here; a narrow tunnel runs south, branching east and west.

*Ghostly Chanting:* audible from arrival, impossible to source until area 26 or 29, words indiscernible until area 35.

**23. Family Crypts.** Sealed with stone slabs (DC 15 Str Athletics to remove; crowbar grants advantage).
- **23A. Empty Crypt.** Slab unsealed and leaning nearby. Empty.
- **23B. Walter's Crypt.** Slab reads "Walter Durst." Empty.
- **23C. Gustav's Crypt.** Slab reads "Gustav Durst." Empty coffin on a stone bier.
- **23D. Elisabeth's Crypt.** Slab reads "Elisabeth Durst." Empty coffin on a bier — a swarm of insects (centipedes) bursts from the back wall if the coffin is disturbed.
- **23E. Rose's Crypt.** Slab reads "Rosavalda Durst." Empty coffin on a bier. Placing Rose's remains (area 20) here lays her ghost to rest permanently (and frees a possessed character).
- **23F. Thorn's Crypt.** Slab reads "Thornboldt Durst." Empty coffin on a bier. Same as above for Thorn.

**24. Cult Initiates' Quarters.** Wooden table, four chairs at the east end; four alcoves to the west with moldy straw pallets.

**25. Well and Cultist Quarters.** 4-ft.-diameter well shaft, 3-ft. lip, 30 ft. deep to a water cistern, rope-pulley bucket. Five side rooms (former senior cultist quarters): wood-framed bed, moldy mattress, locked wooden chest each (DC 15 Dex with thieves' tools).
- **25A.** 11 gp, 60 sp in a pouch made of human skin.
- **25B.** Three moss agates (10 gp each) in folded black cloth.
- **25C.** Black leather eyepatch with a carnelian sewn in (50 gp).
- **25D.** Ivory hairbrush with silver bristles (25 gp).
- **25E.** A silvered shortsword (110 gp).

**26. Hidden Spiked Pit.** Chanting grows louder heading west. DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) notices an absence of footprints. Hidden pit under rotted planks/dirt: 5 ft. long, 10 ft. deep, spiked bottom. First to step on the cover falls, prone, 3 (1d6) bludgeoning + 11 (2d10) piercing damage.

**27. Dining Hall.** Plain wooden table, long benches, moldy humanoid bones on the dirt floor. Darkened alcove in the south wall (area 28) — approaching within 5 ft. provokes its occupant.

**28. Larder.** A grick lurks in the alcove, attacking the first character within 5 ft. it sees. Passive Perception under 12 = surprised. Otherwise empty.

**29. Ghoulish Encounter.** Chanting louder to the north. At the four-way tunnel intersection's midpoint, four ghouls (former cultists) rise from marked spaces and attack to the death.

**30. Stairs Down.** A 20-ft. staircase; obviously the source direction of the chanting below. Descending and following the hall leads to area 35.

## Areas 31–38

**31. Darklord's Shrine.** Moldy skeletons hang from rusty wall shackles (harmless decor). South-wall alcove holds a painted wooden statue of a gaunt, pale-faced man in a black cloak, hand on a wolf's head, holding a smoky-gray crystal orb (Strahd, to whom the cultists sacrificed in hope of secrets). Touching the statue or taking the orb summons five shadows (former cultists' spirits) that attack and pursue fleeing characters beyond the room. Exits west and north; chanting from the west.

*Concealed Door:* middle of the east wall, DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) — an ordinary rotted door hidden under clay, opening to a 10-ft. stone staircase up to a landing (area 32).

*Treasure:* the crystal orb (25 gp, usable as a non-magical arcane focus).

**32. Hidden Trapdoor.** Landing with a 6-ft. plank ceiling and a bolted-shut trapdoor (from this side) that opens to the den (area 3) above. Once found/opened, stays available as a route in and out.

**33. Cult Leaders' Den.** The southwest door is a mimic in disguise — adheres and attacks on touch, or if damaged. Chandelier above a table with two high-backed chairs, an empty clay jug, two clay flagons. Iron candlesticks in two corners, candles long melted away.

**34. Cult Leaders' Quarters.** Large bed with rotted feather mattress, wardrobe of old robes, iron candlesticks, an open crate (thirty torches, a leather sack of fifteen candles). Unlocked footlocker at the bed's foot. Two ghasts (Gustav and Elisabeth Durst, in tattered black robes) hide in wall cavities (marked X) and attack if the footlocker's contents are disturbed.

*Treasure (footlocker):* a folded *cloak of protection*, a small unlocked wooden coffer with four *potions of healing*, a chain shirt, a mess kit, a flask of alchemist's fire, a bullseye lantern, thieves' tools, and a yellow-leather spellbook containing: 1st level — *disguise self*, *identify*, *mage armor*, *magic missile*, *protection from evil and good*; 2nd level — *darkvision*, *hold person*, *invisibility*, *magic weapon*. (Taken from adventurers previously captured and killed by the cult.)

**35. Reliquary.** The ghostly chant (from area 38) is loud here — a dozen or so voices repeating "He is the Ancient. He is the Land." Thirteen wall niches hold worthless cult "relics": a mummified goblin hand on a rope, a knife carved from human bone, a dagger with a rat-skull pommel, an 8-in. varnished orb made from a nothic's eye, a bone aspergillum, a cloak stitched from ghoul skin, a desiccated frog on a stick (resembles a wand of polymorph), a bag of bat guano, a hag's severed finger, a 6-in. wooden mummy figurine, an iron devil-face pendant, a shrunken halfling head, a coffer with a dire wolf's withered tongue. The southernmost tunnel slopes down into murky water, ending at a rusty portcullis (area 37).

**36. Prison.** Rusty shackles on alcove back walls (prisoners long gone — their bones are in area 27).

*Secret Door:* south wall, DC 15 Wisdom (Perception), opens to area 38.

*Treasure:* a human skeleton in a tattered black robe hangs on the back wall of the marked cell (a cult member who doubted the cult) — a gold ring (25 gp) on one finger.

**37. Portcullis.** Blocks the tunnel; DC 20 Strength (Athletics) to force open, or raised/lowered via a wheel in area 38's east wall (out of reach from this side). Floor submerged under 2 ft. of murky water.

**38. Ritual Chamber.** Sunken 40-ft.-square room, smooth acoustic masonry walls, featureless stone pillars, 2 ft. of murky water on most of the floor. A breach in the west wall leads to a refuse-heaped dark cave. Stairs lead to dry ledges (5 ft. high) and a central octagonal dais (also 5 ft. high) with a stone altar carved with grasping ghouls and stained with dry blood. Rusty 8-ft. chains with shackles dangle from the 16-ft. ceiling above the altar (cultists would suspend and bleed victims here). The chanting stops the moment characters arrive.

East wall: a half-embedded wooden wheel connected to the area 37 portcullis mechanism.

West wall breach: a naturally formed alcove whose refuse pile is a sleeping shambling mound (the cultists called it "Lorghoth the Decayer") — DC 15 Intelligence (Nature) to recognize it up close. Wakes if attacked, or if the "One Must Die" ritual is refused after being invoked.

*"One Must Die!"* — If a character climbs the dais, thirteen harmless (undamageable, unturnable, undispellable) apparitions of black-robed, faceless, black-torch-bearing figures appear on the ledges, chanting "One must die!" A character on the dais can determine (DC 11 Int Religion or Wis Insight) that a creature must die on the altar as a sacrifice, or face the cult's wrath — any creature qualifies, illusions don't fool them.
- **If sacrificed:** the apparitions fade, the "He is the Ancient, He is the Land" chant resumes distantly, Strahd is aware, and Death House stops hindering the party (see Endings).
- **If refused** (party leaves the dais without sacrificing): the chant shifts to "Lorghoth the Decayer, we awaken thee!" — the shambling mound wakes and attacks, pursuing through the dungeon (though not beyond it), able to move through tunnels without squeezing. At the start of its first turn the chant shifts again: "The end comes! Death, be praised!" If it dies, the chanting stops and the apparitions vanish forever.

**Endings.** The mists around Death House persist until the party reaches the dais and either appeases or defies the cult; Strahd is satisfied either way, and the mists recede afterward.

- **Cult Appeased:** the house lets the party leave freely once the sacrifice is made. Advance to 3rd level upon emerging.
- **Cult Denied:** if the shambling mound is destroyed or escaped, Death House attacks the party as they try to leave — roll initiative, and the house has transformed:
  - All windows bricked up; bricked windows and outer walls are impervious to weapon attacks and damage spells.
  - All doors replaced with slashing scythe-blade traps: DC 15 Dex (Acrobatics) to pass unscathed, or 1 minute studying + DC 15 Int to time it; failing either deals 2d10 slashing damage while still passing through. A creature pushed through must succeed on a DC 15 Dex save or take the damage. Can't be disarmed.
  - Every room with a fireplace/oven/stove fills with poisonous black smoke (heavily obscured; DC 10 Con save each turn started in it or take 1d10 poison damage).
  - Interior walls become rotted/brittle (AC 5, 5 HP per 5-ft. section, or DC 10 Str Athletics to destroy) — destroying a section releases a swarm of rats that attacks and won't leave the house.
  - Track initiative as the party makes their way out. Once they escape, advance to 3rd level; the house does no further harm.
