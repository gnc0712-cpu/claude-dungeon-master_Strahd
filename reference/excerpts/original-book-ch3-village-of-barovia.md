# Original *Curse of Strahd* — Chapter 3: The Village of Barovia

Player-provided excerpt. Reloaded's Arc B (`Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia.md`) rewrites the *framing* of these scenes substantially (Ismark already knows the party, Kolyan is alive and Ireena unbitten at first arrival, Alenka's mob scene is new, fire-bottle defense of the eastern barricade replaces a quieter arrival, etc.) — always run Reloaded's version of the scene first and use this only for physical descriptions, NPC stats/roleplay notes, and shop/room mechanics Reloaded doesn't restate. Where the two conflict on story facts (e.g., "the burgomaster is already dead" here vs. alive in Reloaded), **Reloaded's version is authoritative** per the campaign's Reloaded-over-original-book rule.

**Context note:** Strahd's beloved Tatyana's soul lives on in Ireena Kolyana, who looks exactly like her; Strahd intends to make Ireena his bride and a vampire, entombed in the castle crypts forever. This is a closely-held secret fact — never state it in narration; Ireena doesn't know it either.

## Approaching the Village

> Tall shapes loom out of the dense fog that surrounds everything. The muddy ground underfoot gives way to slick, wet cobblestones. The tall shapes become recognizable as village dwellings. The windows of each house stare out from pools of blackness. No sound cuts the silence except for mournful sobbing that echoes through the streets from a distance.

The sobbing is Mad Mary (area E3). Except for E1 and E2, village shops are permanently closed and looted of anything valuable; claw marks cover most walls. When the fog eventually burns off, Castle Ravenloft looms overhead "like a lance piercing the sky."

**House Occupants** (roll d20 for any residence besides E3/E4): 1–3 none; 4–8 2d4 swarms of rats (Strahd's servants, house looks abandoned, attack if entered); 9–16 Barovian villagers (1d4 adults + 1d8−1 children — muffled whispers heard at the door, uninterested in strangers, never attack first, flee if possible, cower by candlelight with makeshift holy symbols at night); 17–20 2d4 Strahd zombies (stench of death on opening a door/shutter; converge on the party if they enter).

## E1. Bildrath's Mercantile

> The sparse light from this building spills out from behind drawn heavy curtains. A sign over the door, creaking on its hinges, reads "Bildrath's Mercantile."

70 ft. x 40 ft. Owner **Bildrath Cantemir** (LN human commoner) sells only Adventuring Gear-table items priced under 25 gp, at **10x** listed price (Reloaded's B5e changes this pricing — see the arc file). Trades with passing Vistani; no sanctuary offered, no bargaining ("If you want it badly enough, you'll pay for it."); no competition in the village.

If given a hard time, he calls **Parriwimple** (his nephew/stock-boy, real name Parpol Cantemir — no one uses that name) — gladiator statistics except Intelligence 6, no shield (AC 14). Simple-minded, devoted to his uncle, won't leave the party alone as long as Bildrath objects. (Reloaded gives Parriwimple berserker stats instead — see Arc B.)

## E2. Blood of the Vine Tavern

> A single shaft of light thrusts illumination into the main square, its brightness looking like a solid pillar in the heavy fog. Above the gaping doorway, a sign hangs precariously askew, proclaiming this to be the Blood on the Vine tavern.

~60 ft. square. Sign originally read "Blood of the Vine" (an "n" scratched to an "f" — dark joke). Once fine, now shoddy; hearth fire gives scant warmth. Present: the barkeep, three Vistani, and Ismark Kolyanovich.

**Ismark ("Ismark the Lesser")** — LG human veteran. Sits alone in a corner, sipping wine; called "the Lesser" for living in his father's shadow. Not dour like other Barovians — invites the party to join him, offers to buy their wine, asks their aid protecting his adopted sister **Ireena Kolyana**. If they agree, takes them to the burgomaster's mansion (E4); wants their help escorting Ireena to Vallaki, beyond Castle Ravenloft's view/(he hopes) Strahd's reach — he knows moving her is a gamble but has heard Vallaki is well-defended. Tight-lipped except about Ireena/Strahd — knows what other villagers know, plus that Strahd is inexplicably fixated on Ireena above all others. (If shown a forged "burgomaster's letter" hook document, he can confirm by handwriting that his father didn't write it.) Has trained with weapons for years hoping to one day kill Strahd; will join the party if Ireena is safe first — while traveling with them, counts as a party member for XP division (Reloaded uses milestone leveling instead, so this specific clause doesn't apply to this campaign).

**Arik the Barkeep** — Arik Lorensk, CN human commoner. Pudgy, mindlessly washes and rewashes glasses; takes drink orders in a dull, hollow voice (small wine glass 1 cp, pitcher 1 sp) then returns to washing; ignores questioning. (Reloaded's B5c makes Arik the tavern's sole owner/proprietor instead — use that for Arc B.)

**Vistani Owners** — Alenka, Mirabel, and Sorvia (N human spies), seated near the front door, own the tavern and collect tabs; little interest in the party otherwise. More forthcoming if the party arrives with other Vistani — suggest visiting Madam Eva (Tser Pool). (Reloaded's B5c has these three already fled to Tser Pool before the siege — don't use them as present NPCs for this campaign; Arik is sole proprietor.)

## E3. Mad Mary's Townhouse

> A moaning sob floats through the still, gray streets, coloring your thoughts with sadness. The sounds flow from a dark, two-story townhouse.

~40 ft. square, boarded up/barricaded from inside. **Mad Mary** (CN human commoner) sits on the floor of an upstairs bedroom clutching a malformed doll, lost in grief, barely responsive — will talk haltingly to someone gentle, says nothing if anger is present. Hid her daughter **Gertruda** (now a teenager) in this house her whole life; Gertruda broke out a week ago and hasn't been seen since. Mary fears the worst (justified — see Castle Ravenloft area K42, not yet excerpted).

The doll: strange leer, sackcloth dress, made by Gadof Blinsky (Vallaki toymaker); frayed tag reads "Is No Fun, Is No Blinsky!"

*(Reloaded's Arc B has Gertruda leave for Vallaki the morning of the party's arrival rather than a week prior, and gives a different fate — see the "Mary's News" and "Where's Gertruda?" sections of the arc file. Use Reloaded's version.)*

## E4. Burgomaster's Mansion

> A weary-looking mansion squats behind a rusting iron fence. The iron gates are twisted and torn. The right gate lies cast aside, while the left swings lazily in the wind. The stuttering squeal and clang of the gate repeats with mindless precision. Weeds choke the grounds and press with menace upon the house itself. Yet, against the walls, the growth has been tramped down to create a path all about the domain. Heavy claw markings have stripped the once-beautiful finish of the walls. Great black marks tell of the fires that have assailed the mansion. Not a pane nor a shard of glass stands in any window. All the windows are barred with planks, each one marked with stains of evil omen.

DC 11 Perception (surveying grounds) reveals trampled weeds, wolf paw prints, human footprints (zombies/ghouls under Strahd's control).

Ireena (LG human noble, 14 HP) won't open the barred door unless convinced those outside have no allegiance to Strahd (good roleplay, or DC 15 Cha Deception/Persuasion, or Ismark's presence).

> The interior of the mansion is well furnished, yet the fixtures show signs of great wear. Noticeable oddities are the boarded-up windows and the presence of holy symbols in every room. The burgomaster is in a side drawing room on the floor—lying in a simple wooden coffin surrounded by wilting flowers and a faint odor of decay.

(Ismark and Ireena built the coffin themselves — in the *original* book Kolyan is already dead; Reloaded changes this so Kolyan is alive and wounded at first arrival, dying only during the siege that night. Use Reloaded's timeline.)

**Ireena Kolyana** — striking, auburn hair. In the original book's timeline she's already been bitten twice by Strahd and villagers already fear/avoid her (again: Reloaded delays this to the night of the siege — she's unbitten at first arrival in this campaign). Mild-seeming but strong-willed; helps save herself as best she can. No memory of her life before Kolyan found her, nor of how she came to Barovia; her memories of Strahd's visits are foggy (his vampiric charm) but she vividly recalls "the blazing hunger in his eyes." Believes no one from the village has been brave enough to help bury her father (once he's dead) and asks the party to escort the coffin to Donavich at the church; refuses to leave for Vallaki or anywhere else while her father lies dead in the house.

## E5. Church

Atop a rise against the pillar stone beneath Castle Ravenloft:
> This church has obviously weathered the assaults of evil for centuries on end and is worn and weary. A bell tower rises toward the back, and flickering light shines through holes in the shingled roof. The rafters strain feebly against their load.

At the doors:
> The heavy wooden doors of the church are covered with claw marks and scarred by fire.

Home of the village priest, **Donavich**. Other Barovians shun the church.

### E5a. Hall
10 ft. x 20 ft., unlit, reeks of mildew, 4 doors (2 per side) to adjacent chambers. Beyond: a lit chapel, a soft voice praying — abruptly cut off by an inhuman scream from beneath the floor (the undercroft, E5g).

### E5b. Doru's Bedroom
Dirty, dark. Wooden bed/straw mattress, wooden holy symbol over the headboard. Belonged to Donavich's son Doru (now trapped in the undercroft); unused over a year; nothing of value.

### E5c. Donavich's Bedroom
Dirty. Wooden bed/straw mattress, small table with a burning oil lamp, wooden sun-shaped holy symbol over the headboard. Nothing of value.

### E5d. Trapdoor
Ceiling holed by time/neglect, broken shingles, puddles. A heavy wooden trapdoor in the floor, held by chain and padlock (Donavich lost the key). A young man's anguished screams audible through it. DC 12 Strength to force the swollen/stuck door open (screaming stops once opened). Wooden stairs descend 15 ft. into the undercroft (E5g).

### E5e. Office
Desk/chair against the south wall under a sunburst holy symbol. A bare 10-ft. iron rod on the north wall (a tapestry once hung there). Wooden 4-door cabinet against the far wall. An empty wooden poor box sits on the chair. Desk drawers: blank parchment, quills, dried-up ink. Cabinet: tinderbox, boxed candles, two books — *Hymns to the Dawn* (chants to the Morninglord) and *The Blade of Truth: The Uses of Logic in the War Against Diabolist Heresies, as Fought by the Ulmist Inquisition* (logic exercises mixed with fiend-cult descriptions).

### E5f. Chapel
> The chapel is a shambles, with overturned and broken pews littering the dusty floor. Dozens of candles mounted in candlesticks and candelabras light every dusty corner in a fervent attempt to rid the chapel of shadows. At the far end of the church sits a claw-scarred altar, behind which kneels a priest in soiled vestments. Next to him hangs a long, thick rope that stretches up into the bell tower.

If the undercroft hasn't been visited yet: "Father! I'm starving!" audible from beneath the floor.

**Donavich** (LG human acolyte) — praying all night, hoarse, weak, insane. About a year ago his 20-year-old son **Doru** and several villagers stormed Castle Ravenloft in revolt, lured by a black-robed wizard from a distant land (see area M — the Mad Mage/Mordenkainen). The wizard reportedly died by Strahd's hand; so did Doru, who returned to his father as a vampire spawn. Donavich trapped him in the undercroft, where he remains. Doru hasn't fed since imprisonment and cries out to his father constantly; Donavich prays day and night for a way to save him without destroying him, and tries to stop the party if they seem intent on killing Doru. If Doru dies, Donavich weeps inconsolably.

Additional Donavich lore (beyond common Barovian knowledge):
- Ireena Kolyana isn't Kolyan's natural daughter — he found her, as a girl with no memory of her past, at the edge of the Svalich Woods near the Pillarstone of Ravenloft, and adopted/loved her as his own. Ireena herself never knew this.
- Every night at midnight, the spirits of dead adventurers rise from the church graveyard and march silently toward Castle Ravenloft ("March of the Dead," below).

**Funeral for the Burgomaster:** if Kolyan's body is brought here, Donavich presses the party into helping bury him in the cemetery (E6) at dawn, offering prayers to the Morninglord for his deliverance from Barovia. Afterward, Donavich suggests Ireena be taken as far from the castle as possible — proposes the Abbey of Saint Markovia in Krezk, or failing that, Vallaki. (Donavich doesn't know the abbey has become a den of evil.)

### E5g. Undercroft
> The church's undercroft has rough-hewn walls and a floor made of damp clay and earth. Rotting wooden pillars strain under the weight of the wooden ceiling. Candlelight from the chapel above slips though the cracks, allowing you to glimpse a gaunt shape in the far corner.

**Doru** — a vampire spawn, sent by Strahd to torment Donavich and undermine the church. Starved for blood; brave enough to attack a lone character, but avoids a full group while hissing "I can smell your blood!" — lunges and attacks if his escape is cut off. If restrained and either promised blood or threatened with destruction (or killed and then raised from the dead), he recounts the revolt and his downfall.

*(Card-reading treasure note: if indicated here, it's an unlocked, untrapped, moldy chest in the SW corner.)*

## E6. Cemetery

> A fence of wrought iron with a rusty gate encloses a rectangular plot of land behind the dilapidated church. Tightly packed gravestones shrouded by fog bear the names of souls long passed. All seems quiet.

Peaceful by day. At midnight, the ghostly procession occurs (below).

## Special Events

**March of the Dead** (nightly, midnight): 100 spirits rise from the cemetery (E6) and march up the Old Svalich Road to Castle Ravenloft.

> An eerie green light suffuses the graveyard. From this light emerges a ghostly procession. Wavering images of doughty women toting greatswords, woodwise men with slender bows, dwarves with glittering axes, and archaically dressed mages with beards and strange, pointed hats—all these and more march forth from the graveyard, their numbers growing by the second.

Not the spirits of those buried there — previous adventurers who died trying to destroy Strahd, nightly re-attempting and nightly failing. No interest in the living; can't be hit, damaged, or turned; won't communicate. They march to the castle chapel (K15), up the high tower stair (K18) to the tower top (K59), then throw themselves down the shaft toward the crypts (K84) and vanish.

**Dream Pastries:** the party hears small wooden cart-wheels on wet cobbles — **Morgantha**, a night hag disguised as an old woman (from Old Bonegrinder), selling dream pastries door to door for 1 gp each (most doors don't answer). If observed long enough, she collects payment from one household as a **child** — snatches 7-year-old **Lucian Jarov** from his pleading parents, sacks him, straps him to her cart, and heads back to Old Bonegrinder. She avoids the party if she clocks them as strangers; if confronted and the party demands Lucian's release, she grudgingly complies (intends to return for him later) — fights only in self-defense, and if her life is threatened offers:
- Strahd controls the land/weather; the Vistani are among his spies.
- A Vistani camp lies west at Tser Pool (area G), another on Vallaki's outskirts (area N9).
- Strahd has undead enemies — the fallen knights of the Order of the Silver Dragon, in a ruined mansion west of Vallaki (Argynvostholt, area Q).
- Strahd's most guarded secret: a temple of forbidden lore hidden in the mountains (the Amber Temple), reachable via Tsolenka Pass.
