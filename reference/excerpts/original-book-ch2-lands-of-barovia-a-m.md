# Original *Curse of Strahd* — Chapter 2: The Lands of Barovia (Areas A–M)

Player-provided excerpt from the official book. Covers the valley-wide points of interest between Death House's location and Castle Ravenloft/Vallaki. Reloaded's Arc B cites Areas A and B directly (no rewrite of its own); Areas C onward are provided here for future arcs (Reloaded already rewrites Area C for Arc B — see `Act I - Into the Mists/Arc B - Welcome to Barovia.md` B3 — so use Reloaded's version there, not this one).

## Common Features (applies throughout the Lands of Barovia unless stated otherwise)

- **Doors.** Force open a wooden door: DC 10 Strength check (DC 15 if barred/reinforced). +5 if stone, +10 if iron. -5 if glass/amber or weakened by rot/corrosion.
- **Secret Doors.** Obvious clues (scratch marks, footprints) → passive Perception 15+ notices it automatically. Otherwise: a search plus DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check.
- **Locks.** Proficiency with thieves' tools + DC 15 Dexterity check picks a typical lock. A typical padlock can be smashed with a bludgeoning/slashing weapon on a DC 20 Strength check.
- **Webs.** Ordinary webs/cobwebs can be passed through freely; clearing a 10-ft. square of them is an action. Giant spider webs are different (DMG "Dungeon Hazards").

## A. Old Svalich Road

> Black pools of water stand like dark mirrors in and around the muddy roadway. Giant trees loom on both sides of the road, their branches clawing at the mist.

On foot: 5 hours to area B. Halved if traveling by Vistani wagon.

## B. Gates of Barovia

Two sets exist: one west of the village of Barovia, one east.

> The fog spills out of the forest to swallow up the road behind you. Ahead, jutting from the impenetrable woods on both sides of the road, are high stone buttresses looming gray in the fog. Huge iron gates hang on the stonework. Dew clings with cold tenacity to the rusted bars. Two headless statues of armed guardians flank the gate, their heads now lying among the weeds at their feet. They greet you only with silence.

On foot, the gates swing open (screeching) as the party approaches, and close behind once they pass through. In Vistani wagons: gates open for the lead wagon, close after the rear one enters.

The **eastern** gates don't open for anyone trying to *leave* Strahd's domain unless accompanied by Vistani. The fog chokes any non-Vistani who passes through the eastern gates (or skirts around them while closed) — see "Mists of Ravenloft."

If Strahd is defeated, the gates of Barovia swing open and the road east clears of fog.

## C. Svalich Woods *(superseded for Arc B — see Reloaded's own B3 rewrite; kept here for reference only)*

> Towering trees, whose tops are lost in heavy gray mist, block out all but a death-gray light. The tree trunks are unnaturally close to one another, and the woods have the silence of a forgotten grave, yet exude the feeling of an unvoiced scream.

Vistani wagons pass through to the village (area E) without incident. On foot, highest passive Perception among the party notices "You catch the scent of death on the air" — leads to Dalvan Olensky's corpse (torn/clawed clothes, dead several days, holding a crumpled envelope containing "Kolyan Indirovich's Letter [Version 2]," dated one week prior, sealed with a large "B" — instructing that it be left at the gates to warn visitors off). If the party lingers, a lone wolf howls, joined by one more per round; after 5 rounds, 5 dire wolves attack (joined by 20 more wolves if the party is trying to *leave* Barovia). They break off if the party heads back toward the village.

## D. River Ivlis

> This river flows as clear as a blue winter sky through the valley.

~50 ft. wide, 5–10 ft. deep. Two stone bridges: one near the village (area E), one near Tser Falls (area H).

## F. River Ivlis Crossroads

Check for a random encounter here unless Vistani-escorted.

> An old wooden gallows creaks in a chill wind that blows down from the high ground to the west. A frayed length of rope dances from its beam. The well-worn road splits here, and a signpost opposite the gallows points off in three directions: Barovia Village to the east, Tser Pool to the northwest, and Ravenloft/Vallaki to the southwest. The northwest fork slants down and disappears into the trees, while the southwest fork clings to an upward slope. Across from the gallows, a low wall, crumbling in places, partially encloses a small plot of graves shrouded in fog.

NW fork → river/area G (Vistani lead the party this way if escorted). SW fork → area H. East → stone bridge → village (area E). Gallows platform 5 ft. high w/ stairs. Eleven blank-gravestone graves (rotted coffins, moldy bones if dug up).

**The Hanged One:** as the party leaves, a creaking sound — a lifeless gray body now hangs from the gallows, turning to face them. One random character sees *themselves* hanging there; others see an unfamiliar Barovian. Looks/smells real; melts away to nothing if touched or moved.

*Card-reading treasure note: if indicated, it's buried in one of the graves — cumulative 10% chance per grave dug up.*

## G. Tser Pool Encampment

> The road gradually disappears and is replaced by a twisted, muddy path through the trees... The canopy of mist and branches suddenly gives way to black clouds boiling far above. There is a clearing here, next to a river that widens to form a small lake several hundred feet across. Five colorful round tents, each ten feet in diameter, are pitched outside a ring of four barrel-topped wagons. A much larger tent stands near the shore of the lake, its sagging form lit from within. Near this tent, eight unbridled horses drink from the river. The mournful strains of an accordion clash with the singing of several brightly clad figures around bonfire. A footpath continues beyond this encampment, meandering north between the river and the forest's edge.

12 Vistani (bandits) around the fire, drunk (disadvantage on attacks/checks); 3 sober Vistani (bandit captains) resting in 3 of the 4 wagons, ready to act if alarmed. In league with Strahd but attack only if provoked (threats/insults) — otherwise offer wine and invite the party to join. If the party lingers: "A Vistana's Tale" (below). If in a hurry: a Vistana says Madam Eva foretold their coming and awaits them, pointing to the large tent.

**A Vistana's Tale:** recounts a charismatic wizard who came over a year ago, rallied the peasantry into an army against Strahd, was routed at the castle, fought Strahd in a spellduel that ranged from the courtyard to a cliff overlooking the falls, and was ultimately thrown a thousand feet to his death — body never recovered (river took it). The storyteller doesn't recall his name but it "sounded important." Urges a Madam Eva visit if not yet done.

**Madam Eva's Tent:**
> Magic flames cast a reddish glow over the interior of this tent, revealing a low table covered in a black velvet cloth. Glints of light seem to flash from a crystal ball on the table as a hunched figure peers into its depths. As the crone speaks, her voice crackles like dry weeds. "At last you have arrived!" Cackling laughter bursts like mad lightning from her withered lips.

Madam Eva names each party member and references their past deeds, then offers a card reading. She is cunning, not actually mad; wants Barovia free of its curse; fate interwoven with Strahd's; does his bidding when called on; never angers him or harms the Vistani; never gives aid, never asks for any.

*Treasure table (search a tent/wagon, d20): 1–10 none; 11–13 sack of 100 ep (Strahd's profile stamped); 14–16 pouch of 4d6 gems (100 gp ea.); 17–19 sack of 3d6 cheap jewelry (25 gp total) + 1d6 fine jewelry (250 gp ea.); 20 one magic item (DMG Table B). Card-reading treasure, if indicated: hidden in a wagon; Madam Eva permits a search and it's found.*

## H. Tser Falls

By footpath from G:
> You follow the river to the base of a canyon, at the far end of which a great waterfall spills into a pool, billowing forth clouds of cold mist. A great stone bridge spans the canyon nearly one thousand feet overhead.

By high road:
> You follow the dirt road as it clings to the side of a mountain and ends before an arching bridge of mold-encrusted stone that spans a natural chasm. Gargoyles cloaked in black moss perch on the corners of the bridge, their frowns weatherworn. On the mountainous side of the bridge, a waterfall spills into a misty pool nearly a thousand feet below. The pool feeds a river that meanders into the fog-shrouded pines that blanket the valley.

Chasm walls sheer/slippery, unscalable without magic or climbing gear. Bridge slick but safe. South of bridge → area F; north → area I. Gargoyles are harmless statues.

## I. Black Carriage

> Even here, in the mountains, the forest and the fog are inescapable. Ahead, the dirt road splits in two, widening toward the east. There you see patches of cobblestone, suggesting that the eastern branch was once an important thoroughfare.

If Strahd wants the party steered toward him:
> Parked at the fork in the road, pointed east, is a large black carriage drawn by two black horses. The horses snort puffs of steamy breath into the chill mountain air. The side door of the carriage swings open silently.

Two black draft horses (Strahd-controlled) wait; carriage seats 8; can't be diverted from its course (area J) by any means, including a skilled teamster. Declining the carriage: road NW through gates (area B), or south to Tser Falls (area H).

## J. Gates of Ravenloft

(Text assumes arrival via carriage from I — adjust if arriving otherwise.)

> After winding through the forest and craggy mountain peaks, the road takes a sudden turn to the east, and the startling, awesome presence of Castle Ravenloft towers before you. The carriage comes to a dead stop before twin turrets of stone, broken from years of exposure. Beyond these guard towers is the precipice of a fifty-foot-wide, fog-filled chasm that disappears into unknown depths. A lowered drawbridge of old, shored-up wooden beams stretches across the chasm, between you and the archway to the courtyard. The chains of the drawbridge creak in the wind, their rust-eaten iron straining under the weight. From atop the high walls, stone gargoyles stare at you out of their hollow eye sockets and grin hideously. A rotting wooden portcullis, green with growth, hangs above the entry tunnel. Beyond this location, the main doors of Ravenloft stand open. A rich, warm light spills from within, flooding the courtyard. Torches flutter sadly in sconces on both sides of the open doors.

Drawbridge sturdy but missing boards; each crossing (non-Strahd, non-carriage-horse) has a 5% chance of a board breaking — DC 10 Dex save or fall 1,000 ft.; advantage if an adjacent companion grabs the falling creature.

Green slime clings to the entry-tunnel portcullis (DC 20 Perception to spot); doesn't fall on those entering, but does fall on the first character to *leave* that way.

## L. Lake Zarovich

> At the foot of a mountain, nestled in the misty forest, is a large lake. The water is perfectly still and dark, reflecting the black clouds overhead like a monstrous mirror.

If arriving along the north shore of Vallaki by day:
> Pulled up along the south shore are three small rowboats. A fourth boat can be seen in the middle of the lake, with a lone figure sitting in it, fishing pole in hand.

Each rowboat holds 5. The fisherman is **Bluto Krogarov** (destitute drunkard, in a trance, unresponsive unless attacked), 400 ft. from shore. In his boat, bound/sacked/hidden: **Arabelle**, a 7-year-old Vistana (2 HP, no effective attacks) — descendant of Madam Eva, unknowingly of Barovian-royal/Strahd-connected blood, believes a great destiny awaits her. Bluto kidnapped her (believing Vistani lucky) to sacrifice to the lake for fish. Rescue DC: 15 Str (Athletics) from shore, 10 from a rowboat. If saved, she demands return to her family's camp outside Vallaki (area N9); her father Luvash will reward the party.

## M. Mad Mage of Mount Baratok

(Can occur anywhere at the base of Mount Baratok.)

> North of the mountain lake, the trees begin their steady climb up the slopes of Mount Baratok, its monolithic presence oppressive at this distance. The ground here is rocky, uneven, and tiring to navigate. Even the wolves avoid this neck of the woods. Soon, you climb above the blanket of fog that engulfs the valley. Dark thunderclouds roll overhead. You see an elk standing on a rocky spur about sixty feet away. Suddenly, it assumes the form of a man in tattered black robes. His hair and beard are long, black, and streaked with gray, and his eyes crackle with eldritch power.

The Mad Mage (CN male human archmage, custom spell list below) is secretly **Mordenkainen**, driven mad and memory-wiped by his own *mind blank* (cast on himself, 3d6 hr remaining at time of encounter — after which madness curable normally by *greater restoration*; DC 15 Persuasion or DC 18 Arcana to learn why restoration is failing). Believes the party are enemy agents; attacks with destructive magic, retreats if reduced to 50 HP or fewer ("Tell your dark masters they can break my body, but never my spirit!").

Already expended: 1st-level slot on *mage armor*, 4th on *polymorph* (self), 7th on *Mordenkainen's magnificent mansion*, 8th on *mind blank*.

Spell list — Cantrips (at will): fire bolt, light, mage hand, prestidigitation, shocking grasp. 1st (4): detect magic, mage armor, magic missile, shield. 2nd (3): mirror image, misty step, web. 3rd (3): counterspell, fly, lightning bolt. 4th (3): Mordenkainen's faithful hound, polymorph, stoneskin. 5th (3): Bigby's hand, cone of cold, scrying. 6th (1): true seeing. 7th (1): Mordenkainen's magnificent mansion. 8th (1): mind blank. 9th (1): time stop.

If cured, he invites the party into his hidden extradimensional mansion (invisible door up the mountain) for food/uninterrupted rest. Recognizes other worlds — if told the party is from the Forgotten Realms, asks after "his old friend Elminster of Shadowdale." If not indicated as an ally by the card reading, he declines to join and departs to recover his staff/spellbook, alone (fears the temptation adventurers might feel toward his items) — but leaves each character a *charm of heroism* as a parting gift (DMG "Supernatural Gifts").

*Card-reading note: if Mordenkainen is indicated as an ally, he'll help in a fight against Strahd once cured (not travel with the party otherwise) — but insists the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, Tome of Strahd, and Sunsword be recovered first. Stubborn, listens more than talks; authoritative when he does speak. Never had his own fortune read and doesn't want it. Gladly returns to the party's home world with them afterward if invited, to avoid disappointing them.*
