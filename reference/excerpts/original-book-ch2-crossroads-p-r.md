# Original *Curse of Strahd* — Chapter 2: The Lands of Barovia (Areas P–R)

Player-provided excerpt. For future arcs (Vallaki/Krezk region travel).

## P. Luna River Crossroads

Always check for a random encounter here.

> The road comes to an X intersection, with branches to the northwest, northeast, southwest, and southeast. The lower half of a snapped wooden signpost thrusts upward at an angle near the eastern elbow of the intersection. The top half of the sign, featuring arms pointing in four directions, lies in the weeds nearby.

Rejoined, the sign reads: Krezk and Tsolenka Pass (SW), Lake Baratok (NW), Vallaki and Ravenloft (NE), Berez (SE).

The Old Svalich Road (generally level) runs NE–SW between Vallaki (N) and Krezk (S). ~1/4 mile along the NE branch, a stone bridge crosses the Luna River. The NW branch climbs gently into a dirt trail, rejoining the Old Svalich Road after a couple miles but first branching off to Van Richten's Tower on Lake Baratok (area V). The SE branch follows the river down into a valley, ending at the mostly-abandoned riverside burg of Berez (area U).

## Q. Argynvostholt

Ruined mansion, once a refuge of the Order of the Silver Dragon (opposed Strahd, failed) — full detail in Chapter 7 (not yet excerpted).

## R. Raven River Crossroads

Always check for a random encounter here.

Multiple branches: north → dirt path to Van Richten's Tower on Lake Baratok (area V); south → becomes Tsolenka Pass (area T), winding through the lower mountains along Mount Ghakis; west → toward the Wizard of Wines winery/vineyard (area W), dipping south from road to gravel trail.

At the Old Svalich Road / winery-road intersection, a signpost:
> You see a weatherworn signpost next to the road. The three arms of the sign point along the three branches of the road. The arm pointing north reads Krezk, and through the woods you can see an arching stone bridge spanning a river. The arm pointing east reads Vallaki, and the road slopes up gradually in that direction. The arm pointing southwest reads The Wizard of Wines. The road slopes gently downward in that direction.
