# Blood Hunter Subclass: Order of the Vampire (homebrew)

Source: homebrew Blood Hunter subclass (not an officially published Order — the published Orders are Lycan, Ghostslayer, Mutant, and Profane Soul), provided by the player from their own source. This character's chosen order, taken at level 3.

> A blood hunter of the Order of the Vampire is touched with certain dark gifts. Though they do not fully embrace an unholy transformation like their Order of the Lycan brethren, they harness the power of vampires to become more socially inclined than other blood hunters. Members of this order are able to insert themselves within social circles more easily, using blood to gain knowledge and thereby power over others. Should diplomacy not garner the desired result, their brush with vampirism enables empowered blood curses as well as a unique blood curse that makes them adept at controlling enemies. These blood hunters manipulate to create advantages, then strike with their imposing will fueling their vicious attacks.

## Level 3: Preternatural Charisma

When you join this order at 3rd level, you learn to use your vampiric influences to manipulate your enemies and hunt with the aid of fresh blood.

- You gain proficiency in Persuasion or Deception. **(Open item — confirming with the player which, since the text also lists a "Honeyed Words: gain proficiency in Persuasion" line that may be redundant with this choice or a separate bonus. Not applying either until confirmed.)**
- By placing a drop of wet blood from a specific creature on your tongue, you gain understanding and power over them. If the creature is alive, for the next hour you add your Intelligence modifier to all Charisma checks against that specific creature, provided you share at least one language. If the creature is dead, you gain advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks related to the grisly nature of their death, and Intelligence (History) checks related to the creature should they have a notable past. At the DM's discretion, visions of things unknown to the character can occur on higher rolls.

## Level 3: Vampiric Maledict

You can choose to infuse your Blood Curses with your vampiric powers. When you amplify a Blood Curse and take damage by rolling your Hemocraft die, you can cause the target of the curse to take necrotic damage equal to that die roll, provided it has blood. You then regain hit points equal to half of the damage dealt to the target (minimum of 1) as their blood mingles with yours, and you gain the benefits of Preternatural Charisma against that target.

*(Level 11: the target also suffers additional damage equal to your Intelligence modifier — not relevant yet.)*
