# Warlock (2024 PHB core class)

Source: Player's Handbook (2024), not in this repo's bundled SRD (which is 2014 and, for Warlock, structured differently — Pact Boon is a separate level-3 feature there, not folded into Invocations). Provided by the player from their own copy. Covers base class only, not the four subclasses' full text (Archfey/Celestial/Fiend/Great Old One) beyond what's needed to note Graven hasn't chosen one yet.

**Core traits:** Charisma primary; Hit Point Die d8; Saving Throws Wisdom & Charisma; Skills (choose 2: Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, Religion); Weapons: simple; Armor: light.

## Multiclassing into Warlock (Graven's case)

Per the book's own multiclass rule: a character multiclassing into Warlock gains **only** the Hit Point Die (d8) and training with light armor — not the starting skill proficiencies, saving throw proficiencies, or weapon proficiencies a level-1 Warlock character gets. Graven does **not** gain Wisdom/Charisma save proficiency, the 2-skill choice, or additional weapon proficiency from this multiclass level.

## Level 1: Eldritch Invocations

1 invocation known (see Invocations Known column). **Chosen: Pact of the Blade** (text below). Invocations can be swapped when gaining a Warlock level (can't swap one that's a prerequisite for another you have).

## Level 1: Pact Magic

- **Cantrips:** 2 known — chosen: Eldritch Blast, Toll the Dead.
- **Prepared Spells (1st level+):** 2 — chosen: Armor of Agathys, Hex.
- **Spell Slots:** 1, at 1st level. Regain all on a short or long rest.
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Charisma. Spell save DC = 8 + prof + CHA. Spell attack modifier = prof + CHA.
- **Spellcasting Focus:** an arcane focus.

*(No Warlock Subclass yet — that's a level-3 feature in 2024 rules, not level 1. Graven hasn't bonded with a specific patron identity mechanically; narratively, something took notice of him at the moment of near-death in Death House, but which of the four patron types — or something else — is deliberately unresolved until he actually reaches Warlock 3.)*

## Eldritch Invocation: Pact of the Blade

As a Bonus Action, you can conjure a pact weapon in your hand — a Simple or Martial Melee weapon of your choice with which you bond — or create a bond with a magic weapon you touch; you can't bond with a magic weapon if someone else is attuned to it or another Warlock is bonded with it. Until the bond ends, you have proficiency with the weapon, and you can use it as a Spellcasting Focus.

Whenever you attack with the bonded weapon, you can use your Charisma modifier for the attack and damage rolls instead of Strength or Dexterity, and you can cause the weapon to deal Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage instead of its normal damage type.

The bond ends if you use this Bonus Action again, if the weapon is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute or more, or if you die. A conjured weapon disappears when the bond ends.

**Note for Graven:** his longsword isn't a magic weapon (Crimson Rite only makes its attacks count as magical while active, doesn't make the weapon itself a "magic weapon" for bonding purposes), so bonding with it directly via the "touch a magic weapon" clause isn't available. The "conjure a pact weapon" option creates a new weapon rather than empowering the longsword — worth deciding in play whether that's worth doing versus just continuing to use the longsword/dagger normally.

## Level 2 (Graven's session-3 level-up)

Player-provided, from their own 2024 PHB copy:

- **Hit Points:** class table increase — Graven's new max is 47 (up from 39).
- **Prepared Spells:** +1 — Graven adds **Comprehend Languages**. Prepared list is now: Armor of Agathys, Hex, Comprehend Languages (3 total, up from 2).
- **Eldritch Invocations:** 2 more known (3 total, alongside Pact of the Blade):
  - **Agonizing Blast** — prerequisite level 2+ Warlock + a damage-dealing Warlock cantrip; add Charisma modifier to that cantrip's damage rolls. Repeatable (different cantrip each time). Applied to **Eldritch Blast**.
  - **Eldritch Spear** — prerequisite level 2+ Warlock + a damage-dealing Warlock cantrip with 10+ ft. range; that cantrip's range increases by 30 ft. × Warlock level. Repeatable (different cantrip each time). Applied to **Eldritch Blast** (range now +60 ft. at Warlock 2).
- **Magical Cunning** (new feature): perform a 1-minute esoteric rite; at the end of it, regain expended Pact Magic slots up to half your maximum (round up). Once per Long Rest.

**Not yet confirmed:** exact Spell Slots count at Warlock 2 (the bundled excerpt only had the level-1 row: 1 slot). Assuming the standard 2 slots at 1st level pending player confirmation — flag if that's wrong.
