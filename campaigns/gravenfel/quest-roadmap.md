# Gravenfel — Quest Roadmap (spoiler-light)

Player-facing reference: what each arc is broadly *about*, not what happens in it. No outcomes, twists, or secret identities listed here on purpose — full detail lives in `reference/curse-of-strahd-reloaded/` and stays DM-side until earned in play. Purpose: a wayfinding tool so neither of us accidentally skips into content far out of order (see the Castle Ravenloft near-miss, session 2).

Arcs mostly aren't strictly linear — Reloaded's structure allows a fair amount of player-driven order within an Act. Treat this as "roughly this order, broadly this region" rather than a fixed sequence. Update the "Status" column as arcs open up, complete, or expire.
