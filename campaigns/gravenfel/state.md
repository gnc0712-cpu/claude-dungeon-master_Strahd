## Quest Threads

- **Bury Rose and Thorn.** Promised to the ghost children — carry their remains out of Death House and bury them in the garden outside once he escapes.
- **The Beast.** Something called "the Beast" lives beneath Death House, described by Elisabeth Durst's letter as "the heart of this house." An amber shard can weaken/soothe it if presented while speaking its name (name still unknown). Midnight is a hard deadline tied to it "awakening fully." Gustav visited it personally and came back shaken; Elisabeth called it insatiably hungry.
- **The house is sealed.** Every exterior door/window/balcony is walled off by regenerating fleshy tendrils rooted somewhere beneath the house. No way out until whatever's happening below is resolved.

## Established Facts

- Session Zero held. Solo campaign, full horror content, no prior spoilers, *Lost in the Mists* hook chosen. Campaign named Gravenfel.
- Graven Fel: human Blood Hunter, Haunted One background, Order of the Vampire (homebrew subclass). Bonds: seeks greatness beyond all who came before him; collects trophies of his quarry. Flaws: blinding rage toward undead overrides tactics; believes the ends justify the means even at innocents' expense. The specific horror haunting him is deliberately undefined — to surface gradually in play, not resolved yet.
- Blood Hunter (third-party, Matthew Mercer) is being used for this character — rules text tracked in `reference/excerpts/blood-hunter.md` and `reference/excerpts/blood-hunter-order-of-the-vampire.md`, expanded level by level as needed.
- The house is the Durst family home (Gustav, Elisabeth, children Rosavalda "Rose" and Thornboldt "Thorn"). The family also had ties to a place called "Barovia Village" and owned a mill there (per an invitation found addressed to a "Lady Lovina Wachter") — unknown to Graven, never heard of in Daggerford.
- Klara was the family's live-in nursemaid, beloved by the children. She and Gustav had an affair; she became pregnant, planned a temporary leave, and never returned. A keepsake with her face violently cut out was found, along with a hidden cache (wine, lace marked "K," dried silphium, a note signed "G").
- A letter signed "Strahd von Zarovich" was found addressed to Elisabeth Durst, referencing Gustav's affair and a "bastard son" — contemptuous, possessive in tone. The name means nothing to Graven yet.
- The cult that once used this house called itself the "Priests of Osybus" — their ritual texts are confirmed fraudulent (studied and verified). Whatever real danger exists here isn't from their magic.
- Rose and Thorn were locked in the attic by their parents "to protect them from a monster in the basement" and left to starve; neither parent returned for them. Their ghosts are now traveling with Graven (see Party, above).
- Five silvered crossbow bolts recovered from the Den of Wolves cabinet, plus 55 ordinary bolts and a collection of tarnish-prone silverware from the Dining Room (Graven intends to melt it down for silvering weapons/ammo later — items degrade once actually carried out of the house, per the house's own nature).
- An amber shard was recovered from the Master Suite jewelry box, alongside a letter from Elisabeth to a custodian named "Drasha" about managing "the Beast." The shard has been used to open two sealed doors so far (Library secret room; the dungeon stairwell).
- Open story thread (player-requested, timing flexible): Graven is open to contracting lycanthropy or vampirism at some future point where it best serves the story — not yet triggered.
