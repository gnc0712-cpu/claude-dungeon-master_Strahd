## DM Judgment Calls

- **Solo scaling:** Arc A (and the campaign generally) is written for a 5-person party. Encounters were scaled down by DM judgment rather than run RAW or add a companion NPC — reduce monster counts/HP per encounter to keep tension fair for one character. Apply the same principle going forward into Arc B and beyond.
- **Off-script resolution, Arc A finale:** the flesh mound ("the Beast"/Walter Durst) was not resolved via the book's binary sacrifice-or-fight choice. The player reasoned out Walter's identity from clues, attempted a blood-ritual mercy-kill (improvised, not in Reloaded), and ultimately defeated it in a real fight after the amber shard's soothing effect gave an opening. This diverges from RAW Arc A but is now the settled canon for this campaign — Elisabeth's ghost confrontation and the collapse/escape sequence still played out per the book's script afterward.
- **Graven's Warlock patron is Strahd — confirmed OOC to the player, secret in-fiction.** Player-requested design: the entity that "answered the call" during the flesh mound fight (see Quest Threads) is Strahd von Zarovich himself, sensing a desperate blood-ritual invocation happening inside his own domain. Mechanically, Graven will take **The Undying** patron (Xanathar's Guide to Everything, captured in `reference/excerpts/warlock-patron-undying.md`) at Warlock 3, reflavored as Strahd's own dark gift rather than a generic lich patron — thematically fits (everlasting life at a price, undead-resistance features, path toward Graven's own eventual vampirism). Ties directly into Reloaded's Arc R framing (Strahd evaluates promising individuals as potential generals/consorts) — Strahd is grooming/testing Graven, not befriending him; he remains the campaign's primary antagonist. **Graven himself must not know this** — no in-fiction hint stronger than vague, unplaceable dread until a scene actually earns the reveal (likely around Warlock 3, or a suitably dramatic confrontation). Don't have Graven recognize Strahd's name/nature prematurely from this connection.
- **Leveling plan — targeting level 20, beyond Reloaded's normal ~10-11 endpoint.** Player-requested: since this is a solo campaign (Reloaded is tuned for a 5-person party), extra milestone levels will be sprinkled in throughout the story wherever narratively fitting — not tied strictly 1:1 to Reloaded's per-arc "Expected Character Level" milestones, and not something the player calls at will (leveling stays DM-granted at story beats per CLAUDE.md's milestone rule), but generously paced to reach 20 by the time the Strahd arc concludes. Extra milestones can come from off-script achievements, side content, or simply granting bonus levels at emotionally/narratively significant beats, at DM discretion.

## Established Facts

- Session Zero held. Solo campaign, full horror content, no prior spoilers, *Lost in the Mists* hook chosen. Campaign named Gravenfel.
- Graven Fel: human Blood Hunter (Order of the Vampire, homebrew subclass), multiclassed into Warlock 1 after nearly dying to the flesh mound. Haunted One background. Bonds: seeks greatness beyond all who came before him; collects trophies of his quarry. Flaws: blinding rage toward undead overrides tactics; believes the ends justify the means even at innocents' expense.
- Blood Hunter (third-party, Matthew Mercer) and Warlock (2024 core PHB) rules tracked in `reference/excerpts/blood-hunter.md`, `blood-hunter-order-of-the-vampire.md`, and `warlock-2024.md`. Four Warlock spells (Eldritch Blast, Toll the Dead, Armor of Agathys, Hex) still need their text — see `reference/book-excerpts-needed.md` Tier 0 — before Graven casts any of them for the first time.
- Death House was the Durst family home (Gustav, Elisabeth, children Rosavalda "Rose" and Thornboldt "Thorn") — now destroyed, collapsed into the earth. The family had ties to a place called "Barovia Village" and owned a mill there (per an invitation addressed to "Lady Lovina Wachter") — a name/place Graven has never heard of.
- Klara was the family's live-in nursemaid, beloved by Rose and Thorn. She and Gustav had an affair; she became pregnant with Walter, planned temporary leave, and never returned (murdered by the household's cult). A keepsake with her face violently cut out was found, along with a hidden cache (wine, lace marked "K," dried silphium, a note signed "G").
- **Walter Durst** was Gustav and Klara's child, born and somehow transformed into "the Beast" — a massive flesh mound sustained by human remains, kept fed and managed in secret by the family (Elisabeth left written instructions for a custodian, "Drasha," on soothing him with an amber shard). Graven deduced this from evidence alone (an empty crypt bearing his name, family secrets, an altar reading "FEED HIM") and confirmed it directly by speaking with the creature. Walter's existence ended in the ritual chamber — whether anything of him persists afterward is unknown.
- A letter signed **"Strahd von Zarovich"** was found addressed to Elisabeth Durst, referencing Gustav's affair and Walter as a "bastard son" — contemptuous, possessive in tone. The name means nothing to Graven yet; don't have him recognize it prematurely.
- The cult that once used Death House called itself the "Priests of Osybus" — their ritual texts are confirmed fraudulent. Whatever real danger existed in that house wasn't from their magic.
- **Elisabeth Durst's ghost** confronted Graven directly after the Beast's death, hostile and vengeful, and triggered the house's collapse. **Gustav Durst's ghost** blocked the escape route pleading for Graven to stay and die, but stood aside once Rose and Thorn spoke to him directly.
- Loot recovered from Death House: 5 silvered crossbow bolts, a silvered shortsword, assorted coin/gems, silverware (degrades once removed from the house — already accounted for), 3 blank books, 3 spell scrolls (*bless*, *protection from poison*, *spiritual weapon*), the ancient ritual dagger used to end Walter, and the house's deed/the family's will (curiosities now, since the house no longer exists). Full list in `characters/graven-fel.md`.
- Graven emerged from Death House not in Daggerford but in the Svalich Woods, at the start of the Old Svalich Road — the true beginning of Barovia, though that name hasn't been spoken to him yet. Arc B starts here.
