## Features & Traits

**Hunter's Bane** (Blood Hunter 1) — Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track fey, fiends, or undead; advantage on Intelligence checks to recall information about such creatures. Hemocraft save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Hemocraft modifier. **Hemocraft Modifier: Intelligence.**

**Blood Maledict** (Blood Hunter 1) — Know one blood curse (Blood Curse of Binding, below). Can amplify a curse by taking necrotic damage equal to one roll of the Hemocraft Die (**1d4** at this level, becomes 1d6 at level 5) — damage can't be reduced. Usable once between rests at this level.

**Blood Curse of Binding** — Bonus action, Large or smaller creature within 30 ft. must succeed on a Strength save or have speed reduced to 0 and can't use reactions until the end of Graven's next turn. *Amplified:* lasts 1 minute, affects any size, target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns.

**Resourceful** (Human 2024) — Gain Heroic Inspiration whenever a Long Rest is finished.

**Blood Hound** (Origin Feat, via Human Versatile) — Motion Sensor (aware of Small+ creatures moving within 10 ft. while conscious); No Hiding (advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sound or smell).

**Heart of Darkness** (Haunted One background) — Commoners extend every courtesy and will fight alongside Graven against a threat, unless he's shown himself dangerous to them.
